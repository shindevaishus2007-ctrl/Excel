## E-Commerce Analytics: Swiggy, Zomato, Blinkit .

This analytics project aims to study customer buying habits and cart drop-offs to increase orders and repeat users on Swiggy, Zomato, and Blinkit. It tracks delivery speeds and dark store efficiency to optimize logistics for faster fulfillment. Finally, it evaluates how discounts and delivery fees affect overall profits while comparing all three platforms on pricing, speed, and service quality.

## 📊 Data Description

| Column | Description | Data Type |

| Order ID | Unique identifier for each order | Text/ID |

| Customer ID | Unique identifier for each customer | Text/ID |

|Platform | Platform through which the order was placed | Categorical |

|Order Date & Time | Date and time when the order was placed | Date/Time |

|Delivery Time (Minutes) | Total time taken to deliver the order | Numeric |

|Product Category | Category of the product ordered | Categorical |

|Order Value (INR) | Monetary value of the order | Numeric |

|Customer Feedback | Feedback provided by the customer | Text/Categorical |

| Service Rating | Customer's rating of the service | Numeric |

| Delivery Delay | Indicates whether the order was delayed | Categorical/Boolean|

| Refund Requested | Indicates whether the customer requested a refund |	Categorical/Boolean|

  ## 📝 Conclusion

  ## The E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard
 
  provides a consolidated view of overall sales performance, operational logistics efficiency, category dynamics, and user platforms (Swiggy, Zomato, and Blinkit).
  The analysis shows that the business generated ₹59.10M in total sales across 100,000 completed orders, maintaining a strong average basket size of ₹591 per transaction.     Logistics performance is highly efficient, maintaining a network-wide average delivery time of 29.5 minutes, which aligns directly with the target 30-minute quick-          commerce   delivery benchmark.
  
  Personal Care is the most profitable product category, generating approximately ₹17.40M in sales, followed closely by Grocery at ₹14.19M. Product segments, delivery        delays, and refund behaviors are balanced across platforms, proving that the digital ecosystem satisfies diverse retail and immediate meal demands without heavy reliance    on a single vendor or category.
  
  Overall, the dashboard helps identify high-performing categories, bottleneck trends in order fulfillment speeds, customer sentiment metrics, and platform distribution       splits.
  This makes it easier for operations and category managers to monitor service-level agreements (SLAs) and support data-driven business decisions.
