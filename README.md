## E-Commerce Analytics: Swiggy, zomato, blinkit .

This analytics project aims to study customer buying habits and cart drop-offs to increase orders and repeat users on Swiggy, Zomato, and Blinkit. It tracks delivery speeds and dark store efficiency to optimize logistics for faster fulfillment. Finally, it evaluates how discounts and delivery fees affect overall profits while comparing all three platforms on pricing, speed, and service quality.
